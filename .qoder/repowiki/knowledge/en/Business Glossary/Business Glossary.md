### Pawn（兵）
- Definition：FinSight's internal product philosophy codename. It frames retail investors as Pawn pieces on the financial chessboard — individually vulnerable but collectively powerful when coordinated by precise API-driven 'soldier' agents that provide institutional-grade context.
- Aliases：pawn

### X 光穿透 / X-Ray
- Definition：FinSight's core analysis feature that drills down into fund holdings to reveal underlying stock positions, exposing duplicate exposure across multiple funds and enabling real industry concentration calculations.
- Aliases：底层穿透、X光、X-ray

### 压力测试
- Definition：A scenario engine that simulates extreme market shocks (e.g., stock crash, currency crisis) against the portfolio's underlying holdings to expose fragility before it happens.
- Aliases：stress test

### 动态问诊模型
- Definition：The adaptive AI advisor persona that switches between a gentle 'financial coach' tone and a strict 'financial doctor' tone depending on detected risk levels (e.g., emergency reserve below 3 months).
- Aliases：dynamic advisor、AI 问诊

### Unified Ledger（统一账本）
- Definition：FinSight's data layer that aggregates fragmented assets from banks, brokerages, and third-party platforms into a single normalized portfolio view, supporting multi-currency net worth calculation.
- Aliases：统一账本
